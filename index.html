How it works and what to expect

Files stay between devices

The host transfers the complete file before receivers become ready. Commands include a future timestamp. Receivers estimate the host’s clock from several round trips.

A connection still needs the network

PeerJS provides internet signaling. Direct WebRTC may fail across restrictive networks; a self-hosted relay is optional. A relay carries the audio bytes through that server. YouTube mode contacts YouTube on each device.

Keep the pages open

Phones may suspend background tabs. Keep devices awake and lower the volume before testing. “Ready” means the file and clock estimate are available; it does not prove acoustic alignment.
